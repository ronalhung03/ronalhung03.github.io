## About me

My name is Phi Hung, and I am a research engineer at the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT), Vietnam. I am expected to officially receive my Bachelor’s degree in Artificial Intelligence Engineering in July 2026, under the supervision of Dr. Nguyen Minh Tuan, Head of the Department of Information Security.

My research interests lie in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Evolutionary Computation, with a particular focus on applying evolutionary methods for feature reduction in real-world computer vision systems. Currently, my work spans:

- Machine Learning: classification, transfer learning, and multi-objective learning
- Dimensionality Reduction: feature selection, feature construction, and feature extraction
- Evolutionary Algorithms: particle swarm optimization (PSO) and evolutionary multi-objective optimization (EMO)
- AI for agriculture and healthcare applications
