## About me

I am an AI Engineer and Data Scientist based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, with a Bachelor of Engineering in Artificial Intelligence from the Posts and Telecommunications Institute of Technology (PTIT). My work combines applied computer vision research, mobile AI systems, and experience with banking data and machine learning deployment.

I enjoy understanding research ideas, testing them carefully, and turning them into practical systems. My agricultural vision projects have taught me to look beyond benchmark accuracy: evaluating durian disease classifiers on independently collected field images exposed a substantial gap between test-set results and field performance. That experience motivates my interest in robust models and efficient deployment.

Most recently, I worked as a Data Scientist at Orient Commercial Joint Stock Bank (OCB), contributing to feature stores and production model deployment on Databricks. Earlier, I taught programming and AI at Quanskill and developed components of a Vietnamese legal question-answering system during my internship at FPT Information System.

## Research interests

- Computer vision for agricultural disease recognition and practical field use.
- Efficient deep learning and mobile inference with TensorFlow Lite.
- Generalization under domain shift and careful experimental evaluation.
- Vision-language and multimodal learning, which I am currently exploring.

## Selected work

- **Durian leaf disease classification:** a mobile application and research on transfer learning, particle swarm optimization, and evaluation on independently collected field images.
- **Tomato disease detection and monitoring:** led a two-person project, improved mean test accuracy from 98.4% to 98.9%, and reduced model size from 18.08 MB to 2.76 MB for mobile deployment.
- **Research publications:** work in *Applied Fruit Science* and RTIS 2025, plus a paper on projected bilinear pooling for ConvNeXt-Tiny accepted for publication in *Neural Computing and Applications*.

I am interested in Computer Vision Engineer and Data Scientist opportunities where I can combine applied research, engineering, and business understanding.
