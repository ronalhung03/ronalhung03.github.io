**September 2025 – December 2025**<br>
Individual graduation thesis · PTIT

- Trained a lightweight CNN with particle swarm optimization on 2,595 durian leaf images across six classes. The transfer-learning and PSO work was published in Applied Fruit Science.

- Evaluated models on 98 independently collected, expert-reviewed field images from Lam Dong. Accuracy dropped from approximately 90–92% on the original evaluation to 43–61% under field conditions; MobileNetV3-Large generalized best among the evaluated models.

- Developed an Android application with an optimized vision model and GenAI-assisted treatment recommendations based on predicted disease conditions.
